Certifications You Can Earn

Real, industry-recognized credentials that line up with this pathway. Costs are US list prices at time of writing and move around — always confirm on the vendor's page before registering. Most exams are delivered online through Pearson VUE.

Read this first. There is no single certificate for "self-hosting and serving open AI models." That skill is proven by earning a few credentials together — an AI-concepts cert plus infrastructure and ops certs — and, above all, by your capstone portfolio. The portfolio is the credential that actually shows you can do the work; the certs are supporting evidence.
